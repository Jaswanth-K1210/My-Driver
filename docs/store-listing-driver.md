Draft text for Google Play and the App Store. Review the claims against what
is live before publishing.

## Names

## Short description (Play, 80 characters max)

Drive customers in their own car. Verified trips, weekly payouts.

## Subtitle (App Store, 30 characters max)

Drive for MyDriver

## Full description

MyDriver Partner is the app for drivers on the MyDriver platform. Customers
book you to drive them in their own car; you get there, verify the pickup, and
drive.

**Get trips near you**
Go online and receive trip requests from customers close by. Each request
shows the pickup, the drop, the distance and what you will earn before you
accept.

**Start every trip safely**
Confirm the pickup with a selfie and the customer's one-time code, then
photograph the car at eight points. The photos are time-stamped and sealed, so
there is a clear record of the car's condition before and after the trip.

**Drive within the agreed speed limit**
The customer sets a speed limit for the trip. The app shows your speed against
it and records harsh braking and sharp turns.

**Help is one tap away**
Send a silent SOS to the MyDriver Safety Desk at any point in a trip.

**See what you earn**
Today, this week, every trip, and every payout with its status.

**Keep your profile current**
See your MyDriver score and rating, your badges and tests, your Night Shield
status, and get reminders before a document expires.

## Why the app asks for permissions

Use these in the Play Console declarations and App Store review notes.

- **Location, including in the background.** During a trip the app shares the
  car's position with the customer and the MyDriver Safety Desk, and keeps
  doing so when the screen is off. Outside a trip, location is used only while
  you are online, to match you with nearby requests. A notification is shown
  the whole time location is shared in the background.
- **Camera.** The pickup selfie, the car inspection photos, and identity
  documents.
- **Notifications.** New trip requests, which expire in 20 seconds, and
  document expiry reminders.
- **Motion sensors.** Detecting harsh braking and sharp turns during a trip.

Google Play requires a short video showing the background-location feature in
use and the in-app disclosure before the permission prompt. Record this from a
phone during a test trip.
